How to Navigate the Site

Cooking Recipes (For Disaster): Your go-to hub for authentic Filipino recipes with a twist. Here's how to make the most of the site:

1. Browse Recipes

On the homepage, you'll find recipe cards. Use the filter bar to narrow down by meal type (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, Dessert) and protein type (Chicken, Pork, Fish, Beef, Other), or search for a specific dish.

2. Explore the Recipe Page

Each recipe page has two tabs: the main Recipe tab with ingredients and step-by-step instructions, and a Variations tab offering alternative cooking methods or ingredient twists. Within the Variations tab, use the sub-tabs to switch between each variation.

3. Try the Variations

Every recipe includes some type of variation, whether that's a different cooking method (e.g., grilled instead of fried), an added ingredient (Ginataang Tinola with coconut milk), or a substitution (e.g., using Pechay instead of Kangkong in Sinigang na Baboy). These are designed to help you adapt dishes to your taste or dietary needs.

4. Search Functionality

Use the search bar at the top of any page to find recipes by name. The search works in combination with category filters for precise results.

5. Submit Your Own Recipes

Have a favorite recipe you'd like to share? Use the "Submit Recipe" link in the navigation to upload your creation. The more the merrier!
